Question 1

Where on the body are you experiencing symptoms?

Affects skin on elbows, knees, scalp, lower back and trunk Affects skin folds such as inner elbows and behind knees, as well as face and hands

Question 2

Is the level of itchiness moderate or intense?

Moderate Intense

Question 3

How would you describe the texture of the affected skin?

Thicker, raised rashes and the skin feels scaly Skin feels leathery
