Question 5

How old are you? Usually, age plays a significant role in the diagnostic process.

0-15: Eczema starts in babies and young children, but children will eventually grow out of it with proper treatment. 15 or older: Psoriasis is more common in individuals 15 and older, and it is a lifelong condition.

Dark Skin: purple plaques with gray scales
